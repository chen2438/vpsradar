### Notes

- Hong Kong KVM cloud servers with 1 dedicated IPv4 + 1 IPv6 and self-service management.
- **No mainland China routing optimization is promised, and there are no mainland China routing or latency guarantees.**
- The launch post describes `MWWELCOME` as recurring 50% off for the first 50 units at launch. It also lists `MWDAILY` for recurring 25% off. No calendar end date is stated.
- The official shop says coupons apply to single-server orders; bulk orders pay the unit list price multiplied by quantity.
- Speed is throttled after the monthly transfer allowance. Official traffic-reset fees are ¥19.00, ¥35.00 and ¥69.00 for the three plans, as shown above.
