### Notes

- The code, 50% discount and promotional totals in the table come from the vendor's LET post. The official store confirms plan names, specifications and regular list prices only. When checked on 2026-11-15, the official homepage, blog, VPS index and product configure pages did not display `BLACKFRIDAYEARLY`, 50% off or a Pre-Black Friday banner. This offer applies to Intel VPS only, excluding the Premium Ryzen line.
- The official configure pages confirm monthly and yearly list prices for Nano, Starter and Developer. The official category page showed monthly list prices only for Intermediate and Advanced; their yearly list totals in the table come from LET and are stated as 12 times the monthly prices.
- **Do not confuse the official regular 6-month list prices with the yearly 50%-off totals.** The Nano, Starter and Developer configure pages list undiscounted 6-month cycles at $25.50, $43.20 and $83.70 respectively. These happen to equal the yearly sale totals in LET, but cover a different billing period. The yearly promotional totals here are sourced from the vendor's LET post.
- Location: Buffalo, New York, USA, with Intel Xeon E5-2690 v4, DDR4 and SSD. Test IP: `107.172.136.124`. Individual plans default to 1Gbps, with 10Gbps upgrades available. The official VPS index lists unmetered bandwidth, on-prem DDoS protection and automatic provisioning; the vendor's LET post also lists dedicated IPv4, Linux and Windows, and full root/admin access.
- This is a new campaign separate from Halloween, using the new code `BLACKFRIDAYEARLY`. Monthly savings increase from the Halloween announcement's 20% to 50%, and the free 10Gbps bonus when buying 2+ is new. The yearly 50%-off totals for Nano, Starter and Developer match the Halloween announcement. No end date was published for this campaign.
- The official Budget Intel category also lists Platinum at $52.00/mo, Enterprise at $78.50/mo and Ultimate at $105.00/mo at regular prices, with configurations up to 10 vCPU / 64GB RAM / 500GB SSD. The LET table does not list promotional totals for these plans.
- The official Nano configure page also lists optional extras: additional IPv4 at $1.00/IP/month, a Windows license at +$10 one-time, daily backups at +$5/mo and Pterodactyl installation at +$15 one-time. On-prem 1.2Tbit/s DDoS protection is included.
