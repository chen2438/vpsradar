## GreenCloud 13th anniversary sale

GreenCloud (绿云) announced its 13th anniversary sale on LowEndTalk, stating that **there will be no Black Friday sale this year**. This birthday sale is its only major promotion this season. The official sale page will be updated with restocks and flash deals.

- Promotion period: 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-31 (UTC+0).
- Renewals: the provider says renewal prices will stay unchanged despite rising hardware costs.
- Cashback: during the promotion (2026-10-26 to 10-30 23:59 UTC+0), a single purchase or renewal transaction totaling at least $500 earns 13% back as account credit; balance top-ups do not qualify. Submit a ticket requesting cashback before 2026-11-05.
- All plans are marked "No refund/money back".

### 1313 plan ($123 for three years, i.e. $123 / 3 years)

Shared specifications: 6 fair-share EPYC Milan cores, 13GB RAM, 113GB NVMe 4.0 RAID-10, a 10Gbps port, 1 IPv4 + IPv6, VirtFusion control panel, and 1 free backup/snapshot.

### 2222 plan ($22 annually)

- Singapore DC1 / DC2 and Hong Kong DC2: 2 EPYC Milan cores, 2GB RAM, 22GB NVMe, 750GB traffic and a 10Gbps port; paying for three years doubles either RAM + traffic or storage + traffic (choose via ticket).
- Tokyo, Japan (IIJ): 2 EPYC Rome cores, 2GB RAM, 22GB NVMe and 750GB traffic; paying for three years doubles RAM + traffic.
- Los Angeles / Utah, US: 2 Ryzen 9950X cores, 2GB RAM, 22GB NVMe (RAID-1) and 2222GB traffic; paying for three years in Los Angeles doubles storage + traffic.
