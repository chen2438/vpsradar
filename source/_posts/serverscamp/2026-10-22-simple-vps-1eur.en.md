## ServersCamp Simple VPS launched, from €1/month

ServersCamp announced Simple VPS on LowEndTalk, a new product separate from its cloud platform (standalone servers with local SSD/NVMe):

| Plan | Specifications | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VPS 0.75 | 0.5 vCore / 768MB / 10GB / 500GB traffic | €1.00/month |
| VPS 1 | 1 vCore / 1GB / 20GB / 1TB traffic | €1.50/month |
| VPS 2 | 2 vCore / 2GB / 40GB / 2TB traffic | €3.00/month |
| VPS 4 | 2 vCore / 4GB / 80GB / 3TB traffic | €6.00/month |

### Shared specifications

- Location: Bucharest, Romania. CPUs vary by node: Xeon Gold 6154 / Gold 6132 / Silver 4116 / AMD EPYC 7543.
- Network: 1Gbit/s port, 1 IPv4 + IPv6 /56. Only outbound traffic counts; after the allowance, speed is capped at 100Mbit/s until next month with no overage charges.
- Storage: local SSD or NVMe, up to 10,000 IOPS / 250MB/s. KVM with nested virtualization support.
- Systems: Ubuntu 26.04, Debian 13, Rocky Linux 10, AlmaLinux 10; VNC / serial console and reverse DNS.
- Backups: daily backups cost an additional 20% of the plan price; the latest 5 are retained.
- Billing: card top-ups from €10, with hourly billing. Outbound port 25 is blocked. The provider recommends signing up without a VPN to avoid extra review.
- A "monthly giveaway" also refunds the previous month's Simple VPS spending (a promotion in the source thread, with no end date).
