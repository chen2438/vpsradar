## Zenlayer Black Friday top-up and dedicated bare-metal offers

Zenlayer's NodeSeek and LowEndTalk promoter posts introduce the Black Friday offers; the top-up, cashback and term discounts below follow the official Black Friday page. The server offers cover dedicated bare-metal servers, not VPS.

| Offer | Official promotion details |
| --- | --- |
| Top-up bonus | Top up $100 and the balance reads $150; Black Friday only, existing accounts can top up too |
| New-account first-order credit | $25 back within 48 hours after the first top-up and paid first order; any amount, once |
| Advertised new-account first-order discount | Up to 43% off; the lowest effective rate assumes a new account's first $100 top-up plus the first-order credit, independent of server price |
| Bare-metal term discounts | Extra 15% off for 3 months, 30% off for 6 months, 35% off for 12 months or more; only orders placed during Black Friday |
| S-series dedicated servers | Separate 40% off offer through 2026-12-31 |

### Notes

- The official Black Friday promotion runs from 2026-11-02 through 2026-12-02. The end date's time zone is not stated.
- The official page says bonus balance never expires and list prices return when the sale ends.
- According to the promoter's NodeSeek / LET posts, the S-series discount does not stack with term discounts.
- According to the promoter's NodeSeek / LET posts, the offer is limited to once per account and self-service customers; customers with a sales representative are excluded. The top-up bonus and cashback are voucher credit usable for renewals and new instances, non-refundable and non-withdrawable.
- No specific server models or prices were verified on the official promotion page, so no plan prices are listed here.
