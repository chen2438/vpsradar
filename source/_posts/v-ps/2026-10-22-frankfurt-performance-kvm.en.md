## V.PS Frankfurt, Germany Performance KVM VPS launched

V.PS announced the launch of its Performance KVM VPS series in Frankfurt, Germany:

- Host nodes: AMD EPYC 7003 series processors and enterprise NVMe SSD arrays, with VM backups available.
- Routing: China-optimized routes as promised in section 5.9 of the terms of service. Main upstreams are China Telecom Global CTGNet (formerly CN2 GIA), China Unicom International CUP and China Mobile International CMIN2.
- All plans include 1 IPv4, 1 IPv6 and 2 VM backups.
