### Notes

- The official end timestamp is `2026-12-01T00:00:00-05:00` (US Eastern), equivalent to 2026-12-01 13:00 UTC+8. The page describes the promotion as running “until Cyber Monday”.
- The official FAQ states: “A server keeps the price it was bought at.” When the sale ends, the plans close without repricing running servers.
- Every VPS includes a dedicated IPv4 address by default. IPv6 is on the roadmap and is not available yet.
- The official pricing page includes 2 TB transfer per account plus each server's plan allowance per UTC calendar month, prorated by time provisioned and shared across all servers. Extra transfer costs $3.00/TB per calendar month.
