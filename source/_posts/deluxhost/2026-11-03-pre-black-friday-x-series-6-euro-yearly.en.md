### X Series notes

- Amsterdam, Netherlands, Qupra DC1: Intel Xeon E5-2650 v4, SSD, KVM, PletX DDoS protection and unlimited traffic (fair use). ASN: AS214677.
- X-0 is IPv6-only; X-1 through X-4 include IPv4 + IPv6. X-4 showed “Out of stock” on the official page at approximately 2026-11-03 10:12 UTC+8.
- Eligible annual X Series orders from the LET thread can receive ONE random bonus by posting the Order ID there: +1 vCore / +1 GB RAM / port upgrade up to 10 Gbps. Subject to available resources; existing orders are not eligible.
- `PREBF26` applies only to Storage Boxes, not X Series VPS. No end date stated.
