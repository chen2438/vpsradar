- LET identifies Montreal as Leaseweb DC in Canada and Ogden as Gorillaservers DC in Utah, USA; the official Canadian listings only say Canada. Looking Glass IPv4: Montreal `184.107.141.25`, Ogden `23.239.96.225`.
