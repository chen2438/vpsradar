- The vendor states all prices are in Euro and include VAT, with no setup fee and no coupon needed. Prices are fixed for the booked term and stay the same on renewal.
- Ends 26 November 2026 at 23:59 CET, equivalent to 2026-11-27 06:59 UTC+8, or when sold out.

- Stock is limited; prices and stock are per the official deal pages at time of writing.
