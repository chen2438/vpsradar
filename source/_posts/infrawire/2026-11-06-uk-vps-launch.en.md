### Notes

- `LOWENDTALK` comes only from the LET announcement and was not visible in the official UK page scrape. New customers only, monthly billing, no commitment.
- **Entry's port is quoted as 2.5 Gbps on LET; the official FAQ/marketing copy says 10 Gbps.** The sources differ; both statements are retained here.
- LET lists root/Administrator access, all ports open except port 25 on Linux, Premium DDoS, 24/7 support, IP changes on request, a 7-day money-back guarantee and a 99.99% SLA.
- The UK joins France, Germany, the United States, Hong Kong and Turkey. LET also announces Hong Kong and USA restocks and a Hong Kong port upgrade to 10 Gbps.
