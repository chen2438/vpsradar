## MKCloud 3rd anniversary × Double 11 limited-time offers

MKCloud announced its “3rd anniversary × Double 11 limited-time offers” in the official “Mkcloud通知群” notice channel, offering sitewide limited-time discounts and private-line resource doubling; the channel description links to the official site www.mkcloud.net.

| Product type | Discount | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metered-traffic products | Recurring 15% off | Select directly when ordering new services; holders of eligible existing products can open a ticket to apply or switch to the discount |
| Dedicated-bandwidth products | 50% off the first month | New purchases only; select directly when ordering |

### Private-line resource doubling

| Private-line type | Threshold | CPU cores / RAM / disk | Bandwidth change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IPLC / IEPL metered lines | 6T-and-above plans | Doubled | Peak bandwidth +50% |
| IX metered lines | 10T-and-above plans | Doubled | Peak bandwidth +50% |
| Dedicated-bandwidth lines | Dedicated-bandwidth products | Doubled | Dedicated bandwidth does not increase |

### Notes

- Products already in an existing promotion are excluded from these discounts. The announcement says discounts are selectable at checkout; confirm the discount in the cart.
- Traffic quotas on metered lines are not doubled. Eligible new and existing products can request resource doubling by ticket. During the campaign, existing products upgraded by ticket to an eligible plan can also apply; each machine can use doubling once.
- Products already on a recurring discount keep it on renewal after upgrading. Product transfer fees are waived during the campaign; no end date is stated.
- Official store range on 2026-11-10: metered and dedicated-bandwidth private lines from Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Shanghai/Xiamen/Quanzhou/Guangdong carriers to Hong Kong (IEPL/IPLC/BGP), Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Shanghai to Singapore (IPLC/BGP), Shanghai to Japan (IPLC/BGP), Shanghai to the US (IPLC/BGP), plus Shanghai dynamic-IP dual-line CN2.
